Plan for Cleaning Service App:
Develop a user-friendly application connecting clients seeking cleaning services with registered service providers.
**Core Features**:
1.  **User Authentication & Roles**: Secure signup/login for Clients and Workers. Worker verification.
2.  **Client Interface**: Service browsing, booking, scheduling, in-app payments, and review submission.
3.  **Worker Interface**: Profile management, job listing/acceptance, earnings tracking, and schedule management.
4.  **Service Catalog**: Define and manage various cleaning service types and pricing.
5.  **Payment Gateway Integration**: Secure transaction processing.
6.  **Location-Based Matching**: Efficiently connect workers with nearby job opportunities.

**Initial Development Focus**: Establish core user roles, the service catalog, and the fundamental booking workflow.
**Subsequent Stages**: Implement robust payment processing, location-based features, and comprehensive UI/UX enhancements.
